# WatchDog / Sentry - Elevator Pitch

## 30-Second Pitch

**WatchDog is an intelligent home security system that turns your laptop into a smart motion detector. When it detects movement, it instantly sends push notifications to your iPhone, records the incident, and lets you watch the footage from anywhere. With built-in face recognition, it only alerts you when it sees unfamiliar faces—so you'll never get woken up by your roommate, but you'll know immediately if someone else is in your space.**

## 60-Second Pitch

**WatchDog transforms any laptop into a powerful, AI-powered security camera system. Here's how it works:**

**You set up your laptop in the room you want to monitor. The system continuously watches for motion using advanced computer vision. When it detects movement, it does three things:**
1. **Sends an instant push notification to your iPhone** - so you know immediately, even if you're not home
2. **Records a video of the incident** - capturing 5 seconds before and 5 seconds after motion stops
3. **Uploads everything to the cloud** - so you can review it later

**The smart part? WatchDog uses face recognition to reduce false alarms. You can "teach" it to recognize trusted people—like roommates or family members—and it won't alert you when it sees them. But if an unfamiliar face appears, you'll get an alert right away.**

**The iOS app lets you view all recorded videos, see when each incident happened, and track how many disturbances have been detected. It's like having a security system that's always watching, always learning, and always keeping you informed—without the expensive hardware or monthly fees.**

## Key Features

- **Real-time motion detection** with instant push notifications
- **Automatic video recording** with cloud storage
- **Face recognition** to reduce false alarms
- **Cross-platform**: Laptop (Electron) + iOS app
- **No subscription fees** - runs on your own hardware
- **Easy setup** - just point your laptop camera and go

## Problem It Solves

Traditional security systems are expensive, require professional installation, and often have monthly fees. WatchDog gives you professional-grade security monitoring using hardware you already own, with the convenience of instant mobile alerts and cloud video storage.

## Target Audience

- College students living in dorms or shared apartments
- Renters who can't install permanent security systems
- Anyone who wants affordable, intelligent home monitoring
- People who travel frequently and want to monitor their space
